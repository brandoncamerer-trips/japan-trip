Things to do

Topgolf is the plan on the table. Wade's closes at 7 PM, so if the range is happening it has to be an afternoon stop — and Mox is three minutes from Wade's, so those two pair up naturally.

⛳ Topgolf Renton

780 Logan Ave N, Renton — at The Landing, a short walk from the hotel
Monday hours: 10 AM – 11 PM

The planWalkable

Bays from about $25/hr plus a one-time player card (~$5). One bay fits up to 6 players, walk-ins are fine, and there's a full kitchen and bar. TVs everywhere, so the Seahawks–Bears Monday night game will be on.
