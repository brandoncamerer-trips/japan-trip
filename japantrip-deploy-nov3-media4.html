Haneda arrival

Arrival instructions

Step by step from landing at Tokyo Haneda (Terminal 3) to Hotel Metropolitan Haneda.

  1. Get off the plane with our bags.
  2. Go through immigration (have QR code ready) — Visit Japan Web QR code.
  3. Go to the ATM in the terminal.
    • Brandon withdraws yen.
  4. Go to the Suica terminal (Welcome card vending machine).
    • Get 4 Suica cards (transit cards).
    • Brandon loads the 4 cards with yen.
    • Hand out each card to each person.
  5. Head to the Monorail.
  6. Each person scans their card and boards the Monorail.
    • Use the “Local Train”. The “Haneda Express” and “Rapid Train” do not stop at Tenkūbashi Station.
  7. Get off at the first stop (2 minute ride) — Tenkūbashi Station.
  8. Walk directly to the hotel.
